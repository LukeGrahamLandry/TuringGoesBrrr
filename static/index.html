TuringGoesBrrr

Open Circuit Simulation

TuringGoesBrrr is an online logic circuit simulator. Combine simple logic gates to form complex circuits and investigate their emergent behaviour. Import circuits from Logisim files (.circ) and Turing Complete game saves. A progressive web app is available for offline use.
